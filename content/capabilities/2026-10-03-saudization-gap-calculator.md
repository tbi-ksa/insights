Every Saudi employer eventually asks the same question: how many more Saudi employees do we need to reach the rate we are aiming for? The arithmetic is simple, but it is easy to get wrong once you account for who actually counts, and the answer changes depending on whether you hire or replace.

## What the calculator does

You enter your Saudi and non-Saudi headcount, any roles that sit outside the ratio, and the percentage you want to reach. It returns your current rate, the gap in points, the number of new Saudi hires needed if no one leaves, and the number of replacements needed if you swap non-Saudi roles for Saudi ones instead.

## Who counts

Since 15 April 2026, the Ministry of Human Resources counts only Saudi employees whose contracts are documented electronically on Qiwa towards your Saudization rate. Enter only those employees in the Saudi field. A Saudi employee whose contract is not documented does not count, however long they have worked for you.

## What it deliberately leaves out

The calculator does not tell you which Nitaqat band you sit in. Bands depend on your activity, your size class and the year, and they are set by the ministry. Reserved professions and how your roles are classified can also change who counts. If you want your band position and the cheapest way to move up, send us your activity, headcount and size.
