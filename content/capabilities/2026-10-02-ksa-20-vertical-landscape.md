Every operator in the Kingdom is riding the same Vision 2030 demand on the same regulatory-digital infrastructure. In 2026 that infrastructure tightened on three fronts at once, and the margin it compresses is the margin the demand was meant to create.

## The three forces

**Saudization became a gating constraint.** Since 15 April 2026 only Saudi employees with a Qiwa-documented contract count towards an establishment's rate. Sixty-nine administrative-support professions are reserved for Saudis from 5 April, and sales and marketing roles need 60% from 19 April.

**Compliance became mandatory digital rails.** ZATCA's Wave 25 brings every taxpayer above SAR 187,500 in VAT-subject revenue onto e-invoicing integration by 1 February 2027. The wage clause of a Qiwa-documented contract is now enforceable directly through Najiz.

**Vision 2030 demand carries a governance string.** Industrial, health, tourism and housing programmes lift the top line, and the partners behind that demand increasingly expect disclosure to match.
